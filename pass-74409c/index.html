  3. Nº 01 Heuristics: turn your feedback into rules

    10 minNothing else to install

    Paste the review notes from your last few rounds of client feedback and ask: "Find the feedback that happened twice and draft it as rules." Each draft rule comes with a test. Then paste a Figma frame link and ask for a review against the rulebook. You get pass, fail or needs-human on every rule, with the evidence it saw. It never edits your design.

  4. Nº 02 Studio Ops: triage this week's briefs

    10 minNothing else to install

    Give it your briefs as a CSV export from your task tool (or through the ClickUp connector) and ask: "Triage these briefs." It lists what can't start and the exact field missing, orders the rest by design due date then send date, and marks each one P1 Match or P2 Skeleton. Add a status log and it shows where time actually goes, with a source on every number. You approve the plan; it never reassigns anything on its own.

  5. Nº 03 Model Review: check your AI reviewer

    20 minNothing else to install

    Once an AI reviews designs for you, this team measures it. Give it the AI's comments with your accept or reject on each, and what a human caught on their own. It reports two numbers together: how many AI comments you accepted, and how many of the human's catches the AI also raised, plus the list of what it missed. Under 30 designs it calls the result a pilot.
